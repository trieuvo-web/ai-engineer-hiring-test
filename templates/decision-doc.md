## How to use this template

For each decision below:
- Describe the situation in your own words (1–2 sentences)
- List at least 2 options you considered
- Explain which option you chose and why (focus on trade-offs, not just "it was simpler")
- Optionally: what would you do differently with more time?

Target length: 500–800 words total across all 3 decisions.
